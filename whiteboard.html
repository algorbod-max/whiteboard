How to use the whiteboard

✨ Smart — write or draw with the pen, then pause one second: handwriting becomes typed text, a rough circle, square, triangle or line becomes a clean one, and for drawings a bar offers clean pictures. Click another guess in the bar, or Keep mine to keep exactly what you drew. Default = nothing changes.
Type — click anywhere and type. Enter = new line, Esc = finished. Or just start typing: it goes on the next line. Click something you wrote to change it.
Pen / Mark — draw with the touchpad or mouse. Hold Shift for a straight line.
Erase — click or drag over things. ✥ Move — drag things; drag the corner square to resize a picture; select something and press a colour or size to change it, Delete to remove it; drag an empty spot to slide the board.
Pictures — shapes, stick figures, faces, bubbles and objects: click one to put it in the middle of the screen.
Scroll — the board gets bigger than the screen: two-finger swipe on the touchpad (or mouse wheel; Shift+wheel = sideways). The little map at the bottom left shows where you are.
Timer — choose what it is for and how long; it counts down in the corner and rings at the end.
Start lesson — type the course and session; the red End lesson button stays in the top-right corner. When you press it, every page of the lesson is saved as one picture in WHITEBOARD\Sessions.
